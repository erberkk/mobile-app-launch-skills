# Turkey: App Store and Google Play income under Income Tax Law (GVK) article 20/B

> Not legal or tax advice. Rates, limits and conditions are set by law and secondary regulation and **change, usually every year**. Verify the current rules with the Revenue Administration (GİB, [gib.gov.tr](https://www.gib.gov.tr)), your bank and a certified accountant (SMMM) before relying on any of this.

## What it is

Article 20/B (mükerrer 20/B) of the Turkish Income Tax Law No. 193 provides an income-tax exemption, for **individual** (income-tax) taxpayers, on earnings from mobile applications developed and sold or offered through electronic app sharing and sales platforms (such as the App Store and Google Play). Corporate taxpayers cannot use it. Income from apps distributed outside such platforms is not covered.

The implementing rules are in the General Communiqués on Income Tax (Gelir Vergisi Genel Tebliği) Serial No. 318 and its later amendments (for example Serial No. 325). Read the current consolidated text.

## How it works (general shape)

1. **Exemption certificate.** Apply to your tax office for the exemption certificate (istisna belgesi).
2. **Dedicated bank account.** Open an account at a bank established in Turkey for this activity (or designate one as the rules allow) and collect the store income **only through that account**.
3. **Notify the tax office** of the bank, branch and IBAN within the period the rules specify.
4. **Withholding by the bank.** The bank withholds income tax on the gross amounts credited to the account (the statutory rate has been 15%; the President may change it). You do not deduct expenses.
5. **Final tax within the limit.** If the conditions are met and your gross annual revenue from this activity stays within the annual limit, the withholding is the final tax and this income is not declared in an annual return.
6. **The annual limit** is tied to an income bracket of the income tax tariff (article 103), which is **updated every year**. Look up the current year's amount; do not reuse last year's.

## If the limit is exceeded or a condition is not met

- The exemption does not apply for that year; the income is declared in the annual return and the amounts withheld are credited against the tax computed.
- Banks do not monitor the limit; withholding continues. Track your own totals.
- Tax under-assessed because of unmet conditions can be collected with a tax-loss penalty and late-payment interest.

## Practical checklist

- [ ] Confirm with an accountant that 20/B fits your situation (individual, app sold through a store, no other conflicting status).
- [ ] Obtain the exemption certificate.
- [ ] Open or designate the dedicated account; give its IBAN to Apple (Agreements, Tax, and Banking) and to your Google payments profile.
- [ ] Notify the tax office of the account within the deadline.
- [ ] Keep store payout reports and bank statements per year.
- [ ] Each January, check the new year's limit and rate.
- [ ] Separately, check social security (SGK) and any other obligations with your accountant; 20/B is about income tax only.
- [ ] US tax form for the stores: as a Turkish resident individual you typically complete W-8BEN; whether a treaty claim applies to a given income type is a question for your adviser.
